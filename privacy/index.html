Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

glfbet is an app for setting up golf games and friendly bets with people you already know. This page explains what the app collects, why, and what happens to it.

The short version. We collect only what we need to sign you in, keep your golfer profile, and help you find friends who already use glfbet. Your phone's contacts are never sent to us in readable form. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and the app has no tracking built into it.

How we handle your Contacts

If you give glfbet permission to see your Contacts, the app reads them on your phone and turns each phone number into a scrambled code. Only those codes are sent to us. The actual names and phone numbers never leave your phone, and we never see or store them.

The scrambling only works one way. We can check whether two codes match, but we can't work backwards from a code to a phone number.

If you later turn off Contacts permission, we delete the codes we're holding for you straight away.

One thing we do store as-is

If you invite someone by typing in their email address, we save that address, because we need it to send them the invite. Phone numbers you type in get scrambled; email addresses can't be, or the invite couldn't be delivered.

What we don't collect

  • We don't track your location.
  • We don't collect the names or numbers in your Contacts.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • There is no analytics or tracking software in the app.
  • We never see your password — whoever you sign in with handles that.

How we keep it safe

Your information is stored on Microsoft Azure and is encrypted, both while it's being sent and while it's stored. The database only lets you reach your own information. On your phone, the app keeps your sign-in details in the phone's secure storage — the same protected area your other apps use for passwords and keys.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your information and we don't share it for advertising. A few services handle your information purely to make the app work:

We also collect basic technical information about errors and performance, so we can tell when something is broken. It isn't used to build a picture of you.

Other golfers only see your name and whatever profile details you chose to fill in — and only once you're connected as friends or they've invited you.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. Delete your account and it all goes immediately — see Delete My Data. Your Contacts codes are also deleted as soon as you turn off Contacts permission, even if you keep your account.

What you can do

  • Get a copy. You can download everything we hold about you from inside the app, any time.
  • Delete everything. You can delete your account in the app. It happens straight away and can't be undone.
  • Turn off Contacts. Switch the permission off and the codes we hold are deleted. Nothing else about your account changes.
  • Fix mistakes. You can edit your profile whenever you like.

Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to complain to a data protection regulator.

Children

glfbet isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Where your information is stored

The app runs in Microsoft Azure data centres. If you use glfbet from another part of the world, your information is sent to and stored in the region where the app runs.

Changes to this policy

If this changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page and let you know in the app.
