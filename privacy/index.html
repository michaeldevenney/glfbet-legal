Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

glfbet is a mobile app for arranging golf games and friendly wagers with people you already know. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why it collects it, and what happens to it. It describes the app as actually built — not a generic template.

The short version. We collect the minimum needed to let you sign in, keep a golfer profile, and find friends who already use glfbet. Your phone contacts are never sent to us in readable form. We do not sell your data, we run no advertising, and the app contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Who is responsible

glfbet is an independent project operated by its developer, not a company. For any privacy question or request, contact devenneymike@gmail.com.

How contact matching works

If you grant contacts permission, the app reads your address book on your device, normalises each phone number, and converts it into a one-way hash. Only those hashes are transmitted. Names, raw phone numbers, and email addresses from your address book never leave your phone and are never stored by us. We compare the hashes we receive against hashes of registered users to find matches.

If you later revoke contacts permission, the hashes we hold for you are deleted immediately on the next sync — not batched or deferred.

One case where an email address is stored directly

If you invite someone by typing their email address manually, we store that address as entered, because we need it to deliver the invite. Phone numbers entered manually are hashed; email addresses are not. If you would prefer an invite you sent to be removed, contact us and we will delete it.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect the names or raw numbers in your address book.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and we show no ads.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, attribution, or tracking SDKs.
  • We never receive your password. Sign-in is handled entirely by your chosen provider.

How your data is protected

Data is stored in Microsoft Azure and encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to your records is constrained at the database level so that a signed-in user can reach only their own data. On your phone, session tokens are held in the operating system's secure storage — iOS Keychain or Android Keystore — never in plain files.

Who your data is shared with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. Your data passes through a small number of service providers strictly to operate the app:

We also collect operational telemetry — error and performance data about the backend service itself — through Microsoft Azure Monitor. This is used to keep the service running and is not used to build a profile of you.

Other golfers see only what you would expect them to see: your display name and the profile details you chose to fill in, and only once you are connected as friends or they have invited you.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, contact hashes, friend connections, and device tokens are permanently removed immediately — see Delete My Data. Contact hashes are also removed as soon as you revoke contacts permission, without deleting your account.

Your rights

  • Access and export. You can export your personal data — profile, friend connections, and contact hashes — from within the app at any time.
  • Deletion. You can delete your account and its data from within the app. The action is immediate and permanent.
  • Withdraw contacts access. Revoking contacts permission purges the hashes we hold, without affecting the rest of your account.
  • Correction. You can edit your profile details in the app at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise anything not covered by the in-app controls, email us.

Children

glfbet is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

International transfers

The service is hosted in Microsoft Azure data centres. If you use glfbet from outside the region where it is hosted, your data will be transferred to and processed in that region.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects how your data is used, notify you in the app.
