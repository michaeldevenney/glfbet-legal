Delete My Data

Last updated 17 August 2026

You can delete your glfbet account, and everything we hold about you, from inside the app. There's no form to fill in and no waiting.

How to delete your account

  1. Open glfbet and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. We'll ask you twice on purpose, because this can't be undone.
Deleting is permanent and immediate. There's no waiting period and no way to get it back. If you want a copy of anything, save it first.

Save a copy first

On the same Settings screen, tap Export my data. You'll get a file with your profile, your friends list, and your saved Contacts codes, which you can then save or send wherever you want.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name, photo, home course, handicap, preferred tees, and your setting for who can invite you.
  • The scrambled code for your phone number.
  • All the Contacts codes saved from your phone.
  • Your friend connections.
  • The code used to send notifications to your phone.
  • Your sign-in account.

What might stay

Invites you sent to other people may still show up in their history, because they're part of that person's record as much as yours.

We also keep backups for a short time as a normal safety measure. These expire on their own, and nothing deleted is ever put back into the app from a backup.

Just want to turn off Contacts?

If you only want glfbet to stop matching your Contacts, turn off the Contacts permission in your phone's settings. The codes we're holding get deleted straight away, and nothing else about your account changes.

If you can't get into the app

If you've lost access to your account and can't delete it yourself, use the support contact on the app's store listing to ask us to delete it. We'll check that the account is yours before deleting anything, and confirm once it's done.
