Delete My Data

Last updated 16 August 2026

You can delete your glfbet account and everything associated with it at any time, directly in the app. No email, no form, no waiting period.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open glfbet and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm. You will be asked to confirm twice, deliberately — this cannot be undone.
Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no grace period and no recovery. If you want a copy of your data, export it first.

Export your data first

Before deleting, choose Export my data on the same Settings screen. The export includes your profile, your friend connections, and the contact hashes stored for you, and is handed to your device's share sheet so you can save or send it wherever you like.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — display name, photo, home course, handicap, preferred tees, and your invite visibility preference.
  • The one-way hash of your phone number.
  • All contact hashes stored from your address book.
  • Your friend connections.
  • Registered device tokens used for push notifications.
  • Your account in the sign-in directory.

What may remain

Invites you sent to other people may persist in those recipients' records, since they form part of another person's history rather than yours. If you want an invite you sent removed, email us and we will delete it.

Backups are retained for a short period as part of normal operational practice and age out automatically. Deleted data is not restored into the live service from a backup.

Deleting contact hashes without deleting your account

If you only want to stop contact matching, revoke the app's contacts permission in your phone's system settings. On the next sync the contact hashes we hold for you are purged immediately, and the rest of your account is untouched.

If you cannot access the app

If you have lost access to your account and cannot delete it yourself, email devenneymike@gmail.com from the address associated with the account, asking for deletion. We will verify what we reasonably can before deleting, and confirm once it is done.
